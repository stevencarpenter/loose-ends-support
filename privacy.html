Loose Ends Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

What the app collects

Loose Ends does not collect your data.

The app has no account, no server, no ads, and no tracking. The app makes no network connection.

The developer cannot see anything that you capture.

What stays on your device

The sources that you capture (scans, photos, PDF files, and text) and the items made from them are stored on your device.

Content that you share into Loose Ends from another app is also saved on your device.

How the app reads a source

Text recognition and the reader run on your device.

On a device that supports it, the app also uses the on-device language model that is part of iOS and iPadOS.

The app does not send a source or its text off your device.

Permissions

Camera: used only when you scan paper.

Calendar: used only after you turn on Apple Calendar in Settings. The app lists your calendars, and it adds, changes, or removes the events that it added. The app does not read your other events.

Reminders: used only after you turn on Apple Reminders in Settings. The app lists your reminder lists, adds the to-dos that you confirm, and reads if you completed them.

Notifications: used for alerts before a due date.

Each permission is optional. You can change a permission in the Settings app of your device.

Calendar and Reminders

Events and to-dos that the app adds are stored by the Calendar and Reminders apps.

If you sync those apps with iCloud or with another account, that provider handles the data under its own privacy policy.

Sharing and export

"Send to someone" and the export functions use the share sheet of your device. You choose the app and the person that receive the data.

Search

The app adds the titles and dates of your items to the search index on your device, so that you can find them with Spotlight.

Backups

A backup of your device can include the data of this app. Your device settings control backups.

How to delete your data

Open Settings in Loose Ends, then tap "Delete all data". This removes all items and captures from this device.

Events and reminders that the app added stay in the Calendar and Reminders apps until you delete them there.

When you delete the app, its data is removed from your device.

Children

The app does not collect data from any person, and this includes children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new text appears in the app and on the web page of the policy, with a new date.

Contact

To ask a question, use the support link on the App Store page of Loose Ends.
