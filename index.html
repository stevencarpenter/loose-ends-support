Loose Ends Support

Loose Ends is an app for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Send a question or a problem report to looseends@sjcarpenter.com.

Include the model of your device and its iOS or iPadOS version. Do not send a source that holds private data.

Common questions

The app found a wrong date. Change the date on the review card before you add the item. The card shows the line of the source that the date came from.

An event is not in my calendar. Open Settings in Loose Ends and turn on Apple Calendar. Then choose the calendar. The app adds only the events that you confirm after that.

How do I move my data out of the app? Open Settings in Loose Ends, then use "Export as JSON" or "Export as CSV".

How do I delete my data? Open Settings in Loose Ends, then tap "Delete all data".

Which languages does the app read? The reader supports English.
